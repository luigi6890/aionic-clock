          Overview

          Aionic Clock is an all-in-one time kit: a comprehensive interactive digital and analog clock fused with a timer, stopwatch, alarms, Pomodoro focus sessions with tasks, a world clock, various background themes and palettes, a fullscreen wall mode, and procedural ambient soundscapes.

          It solves tab-switching fatigue — instead of juggling a clock app, a timer app, a focus app, and a soundscape app, one calm page covers timing, productivity, and atmosphere. Use it as a desk clock, a study companion, a meeting timer, or a screensaver.

          Built for anyone who likes their time beautifully kept.

          Name & inspiration

          Aionic fuses two ideas of time. Aion, the Hellenistic deity of unbounded, eternal time — often depicted ringed by the coils of a serpent — stands for the infinite: cycles, skies, and ambient moods that never repeat the same way twice.

          The atomic clock stands for the opposite pole: absolute precision — boundary-locked seconds, phase-locked blinks, and timers you can trust. The logo brings them together: a bright gradient A ringed by twelve hour markers — four glowing cardinals and eight white orbs.

          How to use

          1. Pick a view: C clock, T timer, S stopwatch, A alarm, P Pomodoro, W world clock, ~ ambience.
          2. Press M for digital/analog mode, F for 12/24H, D for dial styles, / for clock hand sweep/tick, X for the fullscreen wall clock.
          3. Click a world-clock zone to preview it on the main dial; click again to return to local.
          4. Focus with Pomodoro: flip Focus/Short/Long phases, tune the minutes, cycle length and auto-starts, and add tasks with priorities and 🍅 block estimates (click one to activate it). Press the dial for Do Not Disturb; ⏭ skips forward in true cycle order.
          5. Run alarms: click any row to edit time, label, repeat, category or sound; Test fires the true same-time group; drag to reorder, sort, or watch live countdowns.
          6. Hit the 🎵 button for ambient soundscapes — mix layers, shuffle, or play all.
          7. Restyle with L/1 (light/dark), the 🪟/🧱 toggle or 2 (glass/solid), and ☁️ or 3 for living backgrounds.
          8. Press ?/H anytime for the controls and shortcut map, or ⓘ/` to revisit this guide.

          Tech stack

          HTMLCSSJavaScriptWeb Audio APISVGIntl APIlocalStorage

          No frameworks, no audio files, no network calls. A hand-rolled Web Audio synthesis engine voices eight soundscapes through a live spectrum visualizer plus five alarm tones; an Intl-powered zone engine tracks ten cities with DST-aware offsets; a boundary-locked timing loop keeps digits, colons, and hands in lockstep; container-query dials scale every hand proportionally; and a persisted state layer remembers alarms, tasks, themes, and mixes across visits — every sound, sky, and motion generated live in code.

          • Calm technology — chrome fades away; time stays.
          • Procedural everything — sounds, skies, and motion synthesized live.
          • Keyboard-first — every major action has a key.
          • Soft by default — no pure black/white, rounded everywhere, gentle motion.

          FAQ

          Does it work offline?

          Yes. It's a single page of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no network requests — save it and open it anywhere.

          Does it track my location?

          No. World times come from built-in IANA time zones; no geolocation is ever requested.

          How accurate is the clock?

          It follows your system clock, with digits locked to whole-second boundaries so the blinking colons stay in step.

          Can I use it as a screensaver?

          Press X for wall mode — it goes fullscreen and hides every control until you move the mouse.

          Are my alarms and tasks saved?

          Yes, in this browser via localStorage — alarms, tasks, Pomodoro settings, themes, and volume all persist across visits.

          Why didn't my alarm make sound?

          Browsers block audio until you interact with the page — click or press any key once. Also check the alarm is toggled ON (greyed out means off).

          Can I mix multiple ambient sounds?

          Yes — every soundscape layers independently. Press 🔀 for an auto-DJ rotation that swaps tracks every minute or so, or All to play everything at once.

          Do timers keep running in another tab?

          Yes. All timing is computed from wall-clock math rather than tick counting, so Pomodoro, timer, and alarms stay correct even in background tabs (alarms need the page open to ring).

          How do I test an alarm without waiting?

          Hit Test on any alarm row — it rehearses the full ringing experience, pulling in same-time alarms scheduled today, without touching the schedule.

          Why does my world-clock order keep changing?

          Time sort follows live countdown order. Pick Manual in the Sort box to pin your own order with drag and drop.
